Data Deletion Instructions

FaithFlow

If you would like your personal data deleted from FaithFlow — including your account, journal entries, saved notes, and Faith Points history — follow the steps below.

  1. Email us your request. Send an email to info@faithflownetwork.co.za with the subject line "Data Deletion Request," using the same email address associated with your FaithFlow account.
  2. We verify your request. To protect your account from unauthorized deletion, we may reply asking you to confirm the request from the account's registered email address.
  3. We process the deletion. Once confirmed, we will delete your account and associated personal data — including journal entries, saved notes, and Faith Points records — within 14 days.
Some information may be retained beyond deletion where required by law — for example, transaction records related to subscription payments, which we are obligated to retain for financial and tax purposes.

If you signed in to FaithFlow using Facebook Login, deleting your Facebook account or removing FaithFlow's access from your Facebook settings does not automatically delete your FaithFlow account data — please follow the steps above to ensure full deletion from our systems.
