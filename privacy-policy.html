Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026 · Last updated 15 August 2026

FaithFlow Network ("FaithFlow," "we," "us," or "our") operates the FaithFlow mobile application. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. By using FaithFlow, you agree to the practices described below.

01Who we are

FaithFlow Network is a registered company based in South Africa. For the purposes of POPIA, FaithFlow Network is the Responsible Party for the personal information described in this policy.

Information Officer: [Insert Information Officer name]
Email: info@faithflownetwork.co.za

POPIA requires every organisation processing personal information to designate an Information Officer and register them with the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za before this policy is fully compliant.

02Information we collect

Provided directly: account email and credentials (Firebase Authentication); journal entries and personal reflections; messages sent to GRACE (our AI companion) and to our support chat (Crisp).

Collected automatically: reading progress, Bible version preferences, Faith Points history; device and advertising identifiers via Google AdMob for free-tier ads; purchase and subscription status via RevenueCat.

We do not knowingly collect payment card details directly — these are handled by Google Play's billing system and RevenueCat.

03How we use your information

  • Provide core app functionality — Bible reading, audio playback, journaling, cross-references
  • Personalise your experience — reading progress, preferences, Faith Points
  • Process and manage subscription purchases
  • Respond to support requests
  • Generate responses through GRACE
  • Display relevant advertisements to free-tier users
  • Improve app stability and comply with legal obligations

05Data storage & international transfer

Your data is primarily stored on Google Firebase servers, which may be located outside South Africa. By using FaithFlow, you consent to your information being processed in jurisdictions where data protection laws may differ from POPIA. We rely on Firebase's compliance certifications as a safeguard for such transfers.

06Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required (e.g. subscription financial records). See our Data Deletion Instructions for how to request deletion.

07Your rights under POPIA

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent at any time, e.g. opting out of ad personalisation in device settings
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator
Information Regulator (South Africa): complaints.IR@justice.gov.za · inforegulator.org.za

08Children's privacy

FaithFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

09Data security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10Advertising & Faith Points

Free-tier users see banner ads and may opt into rewarded ads for Faith Points, managed through Google AdMob. Limit ad personalisation via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected in an updated date above, or communicated in-app. Continued use of FaithFlow after changes constitutes acceptance.
